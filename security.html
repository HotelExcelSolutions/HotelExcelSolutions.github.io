How we keep your data safe

What the platform does today, in plain words, and what we are adding next. Everything on this page is in place now unless it says otherwise.

In place today

What we do today

These protections are built into the platform at app.hotelexcelsolutions.com.

Two-step sign-in for everyone

Every person signs in with a password and a code from an authenticator app. The database itself checks the second step on every read and every save, not only the screen.

Passwords are at least 12 characters, and passwords known from other sites’ data breaches are refused. You are signed out after 30 minutes without activity. Sign-in and password-reset messages never say whether an email address has an account.

Each person sees only what their role needs

A hotel’s data is visible only to that hotel’s people. Financial figures are visible to the finance team (staff accountant, assistant director of finance, director of finance).

IT and user administrators manage accounts without seeing financial figures. Hotel Excel Solutions manages hotels and accounts and cannot read a hotel’s financial figures. Only the director of finance gives someone finance access. Corporate access is view only, unless a named ability is given for that company.

Approvals, with nothing quietly changed

Staff save their work; an assistant director of finance or director of finance approves it. Nobody approves their own work, except a director of finance, and that is recorded.

Approved versions are kept. Reopening a day or a month needs a reason, and closed months can require approval from the company. Every change is written to an audit log that cannot be edited or deleted.

Your reports stay on your computer

PMS, POS, bank and payroll files are read in your browser. Only the results are saved: totals, journal entries and summaries. The report files themselves are not uploaded.

For payroll, the database accepts totals by department only, so names, employee numbers and individual pay cannot be stored. Files you attach to a request are cleaned of guest names, card numbers and email addresses before they are sent, you check the cleaned copy, and they are deleted 30 days after the request is closed.

Files we store only when you choose to

Invoice copies, vendor W-9s, balance sheet and journal entry support, and corporate appendices are kept in private storage, open only to the roles that need them. W-9s are open to the director of finance only. Opening an invoice copy, a W-9 or a support file is recorded.

For printing checks, a hotel’s own bank account number is kept apart from everything else. No screen can read it; only an approved check run printed by an assistant director of finance or director of finance receives it, and that is recorded.

A locked-down website

The site is served only over HTTPS. It runs only our own code: no third-party scripts, advertising, trackers or outside fonts, and it talks only to our database. Other sites cannot embed it, and it is kept out of search engines.

Where your data lives

The services we use, and backups

The services we use

Supabase runs our database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States (US East). Supabase states that it encrypts stored data; we rely on that and have not verified it ourselves.

Cloudflare hosts the website and our domain. Resend sends sign-in emails and the daily accounts receivable reminder email to your own team; the platform never emails your clients. GitHub holds our code, never hotel data.

Backups and your own copy

Our database is backed up daily by Supabase, and each backup is kept for 7 days. Stored files (invoice copies, W-9s, support files) are not part of those backups yet.

A hotel’s director of finance can download a copy of the hotel’s records at any time, in one file. Full bank account numbers are never in it.

Not done yet

What we are adding next

These are planned, not in place. We will move each one up to “What we do today” when it is done.

More backup and recovery

A separate backup of stored files. A practised restore, with written steps. A one-click download of everything, stored files included.

Sign-in time limits

A limit on how long a sign-in lasts, in addition to signing out after 30 minutes without activity.

Written policies and agreements

Written policies for access, incident response, data retention and the services we use. A privacy policy, terms and a data processing agreement drafted by an attorney.

Independent checks

An independent penetration test. We have not been through a security audit or certification such as SOC 2; if we do, we will say so here.

Regular access reviews

A quarterly review with each hotel of who has access, so people who have left are removed.

Questions about how we handle your data?

Ask us anything. If your company has a security questionnaire, send it and we will answer it.
